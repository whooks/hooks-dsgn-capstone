# Are birds really dinosaurs?

Short answer: Yes. Birds are a living branch of theropod dinosaurs. They evolved from small, feathered, meat-eating dinosaurs more than 150 million years ago, which means dinosaurs never fully went extinct — about 11,000 species of them are alive today as birds.

## The evolutionary link

Birds belong to the theropods, the same group of two-legged, mostly meat-eating dinosaurs that includes Tyrannosaurus rex and Velociraptor. Decades of fossil discoveries show a clear series of shared features between small theropods and early birds: hollow bones, wishbones (furculae), three-fingered hands, specific wrist and hip structures, and feathers. Because of this ancestry, scientists classify birds within the dinosaur family tree rather than as a separate group.

## Feathers came before flight

Many non-bird dinosaurs had feathers or feather-like structures, especially among the theropods. Feathers likely first evolved for insulation or display, not flight, and only later were adapted for flying in the bird lineage. Famous transitional fossils such as Archaeopteryx (about 150 million years old) combine reptilian features (teeth, a long bony tail, clawed fingers) with bird-like feathered wings.

## What "dinosaurs went extinct" really means

The asteroid impact 66 million years ago wiped out the non-avian (non-bird) dinosaurs. The bird lineage survived and diversified into today's birds. So it is most accurate to say the "non-bird dinosaurs" died out, while dinosaurs as a whole persist as birds.

## A common related question: did humans and dinosaurs coexist?

Not the non-bird dinosaurs. They died out about 66 million years ago, and the earliest members of the human family appeared only within roughly the last few million years — a gap of tens of millions of years. Humans do, however, live alongside dinosaurs in the technical sense every day: birds.

## Key terms

- Theropod: the dinosaur group of bipedal, mostly carnivorous dinosaurs that includes birds.
- Avian / non-avian: "avian" means birds; "non-avian dinosaurs" are all the dinosaurs that were not birds.
