# Argentine sauropodomorph sheds light on how long necks evolved

Summary: A newly discovered sauropodomorph dinosaur from the mountains of Argentina may help pin down when and how plant-eating dinosaurs evolved their famously long necks.

## What was found

Paleontologists described a new species of sauropodomorph from Argentina. Sauropodomorphs are the broad group that includes the giant long-necked sauropods and their smaller, earlier relatives. The new animal comes from early in the group's history, making it useful for studying the origins of the long-necked body plan rather than its later, gigantic forms.

## Why it matters

The very long neck is one of the most iconic features of sauropod dinosaurs, letting them reach vegetation other animals could not. By placing a new early sauropodomorph in the family tree, researchers can better estimate when neck elongation began and which anatomical changes came first. Argentina has produced many of the earliest dinosaurs known, so finds there are especially informative about dinosaur origins.

## Key terms

- Sauropodomorph: the dinosaur lineage that includes long-necked sauropods and their early relatives.
- Body plan: the general anatomical layout of an organism.
